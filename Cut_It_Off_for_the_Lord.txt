CUT IT OFF FOR THE LORD

An Unauthorized Sacrifice

The modern church has discovered the sanctity of the penis.

It was a late discovery, but let us not be ungrateful.

To hear certain Christians tell it, the modern world is prowling the streets with a clipboard and a pair of garden shears, forcing transgender people to surrender perfectly innocent genitals to the great progressive machine.

Never mind that we are speaking here of grown adults. Adults who consult doctors, sign forms, weigh consequences, and make decisions about their own bodies. The same grown adults whom the church ordinarily tells to take personal responsibility for absolutely everything are, in this one suspiciously convenient case, helpless victims incapable of choosing anything at all.

Freedom is sacred until someone uses it incorrectly.

Then it becomes coercion.

And so the pulpit trembles.

"They are cutting off penises!"

Yes, pastor. Lower your voice. God has heard of penises.

In fact, biblical religion introduced itself to a great many men by taking a knife to one.

Circumcision was not a secular invention. No rainbow flag descended upon Abraham. The covenant itself arrived with a bodily alteration so intimate that the Almighty apparently chose male flesh as His signature line.

To be fair, it was only the foreskin.

God was pacing Himself.

But the principle had been established. The body could be permanently changed in the name of identity, belonging, and sacred obedience. Not merely tolerated. Commanded.

Strange, then, to hear heirs of that tradition speak as though altering genital tissue is an idea so foreign to God that He might drop His thunderbolt from shock.

I hardly think the Lord is bothered by all this cutting.

Have you read His book?

This is the God whose son advised that if an eye leads you into sin, pluck it out, and if a hand leads you into sin, cut it off. Suddenly Christians become masterful literary critics.

"It is a metaphor!"

Ah.

Now it is a metaphor.

Eternal fire is apparently plain speech, but the moment the verse approaches the deductible, everyone discovers symbolism.

Then there are the eunuchs.

Jesus himself spoke of those who made themselves eunuchs for the kingdom of heaven, which is a rather awkward line for a religion currently pretending that voluntary bodily alteration is incomprehensible to the divine mind.

Paul, never one to leave a delicate subject sufficiently delicate, even wished that certain circumcision enthusiasts would go all the way and emasculate themselves.

The Bible is many things.

Squeamish is not one of them.

Yet the church recoils from transgender adults as though Christianity had spent two thousand years teaching that the body must remain exactly as it arrived.

Really?

This is the faith of fasting, celibacy, mortification, martyrdom, pierced saints, severed heads, and bodies offered as living sacrifices. It praises men who surrendered comfort, wealth, family, freedom, blood, and life itself. Abraham was prepared to surrender his son and became a monument to obedience. The martyrs walked into death and became heroes. Every hymn insists that the truly devoted believer must give God everything.

Everything, it seems, except the penis.

That belongs to the historic preservation society.

The devoted are told to lay down their lives for the Lord, but apparently laying down a particular appendage would be showing off.

And this raises a theological question.

If genuine love of God means total surrender, why should the surrender stop at anatomy?

If He may demand your career, your marriage, your money, your pleasure, your future, and your life, why does the penis alone enjoy diplomatic immunity?

"God wants your heart," they will say.

How fortunate that the desired organ is always metaphorical.

Frankly, by the church's own logic, if you are not willing to cut off your penis for the Lord, are you even serious about heaven?

There you stand on Judgment Day, entirely intact.

"I gave You everything, Lord."

Did you?

The evidence suggests you kept a little something for yourself.

Please do not cut anything off. That is not medical advice. It is theological quality control.

The absurdity is the point.

No one should have to mutilate himself to prove devotion. No one should pluck out an eye to satisfy a verse, surrender healthy flesh to calm an invisible judge, or treat pain as proof that love is sincere. A person's body belongs to that person, whether the church approves of what the person does with it or not.

And there lies the true offense.

The church does not hate altered bodies. It has blessed altered bodies, disciplined bodies, scarred bodies, celibate bodies, circumcised bodies, and dead bodies whenever the alteration pointed toward God.

What it hates is an unauthorized altar.

It is not merely the sacrifice that offends them. It is that the sacrifice was made without their Lord at its center. An adult changed the body for a reason the church did not supply, toward an identity the church did not authorize, and without first asking permission from men who insist the flesh belongs to God.

That is the scandal.

Not bodily alteration.

Bodily autonomy.

So spare me the theatrical horror. Your scripture begins with circumcision, proceeds through severed limbs and plucked eyes, pauses beside eunuchs for heaven, and ends with the faithful washed in sacrificial blood.

Christianity may object to many things.

It has forfeited the right to pretend it finds sacred bodily sacrifice unimaginable.

No, I do not think God is scandalized by the operation.

If the Bible is any indication, He may merely be annoyed it was not done for Him.
